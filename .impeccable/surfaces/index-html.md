# Surface: portfolio home (index.html) + galerie

Mode: Experience. Audience: the tech network arriving from LinkedIn. Job: in one minute, know who Thibault is (développeur logiciel, Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise), see the flagship (chatbot multi-agent), browse the work and photos, reach him. Constraints: static site on Vercel, `/api/chat` assistant inline, FR primary + EN toggle, ALE work confidential.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Thibault presented the way Apple presents a product: one idea per section, a centred headline, generous white space, one large image, and nothing decorative. It refuses themed concepts (film, paper, pencil) and the dark "creative developer" portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: White and #f5f5f7 alternating grounds, #1d1d1f ink, #6e6e73 secondary, Apple blue #0071e3 for actions and #0066cc for links, black tiles for dark media. SF Pro on Apple devices, self-hosted Inter elsewhere. 18–28px rounded tiles, pill buttons, translucent sticky nav, hairlines, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor meets him (name, title, portrait), understands the multi-agent chatbot, asks the assistant, scans the project tiles, sees the photographs, reads the path, writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky translucent product nav (name left, links, blue "Contact" pill). Centred "Thibault Dubois." at Apple hero scale, one-line title, a blue pill "Voir les projets" and a link "Me contacter". Below, the portrait in a large rounded frame that grows to full width as you scroll.

FORM: Apple canon, light product page (the standing exit taken by the user after rejecting the assigned contact-sheet world). seed:02ebb7aa8cca09f2.
